Refund and Cancellation Policy

How cancelling and refunds work.

Last updated: October 2026

Free tools

All tools on ToolKit Hub are free to use, so there is nothing to refund.

Paid plans

This policy applies when paid plans such as ToolKit Hub Pro are available. The price, billing period and payment provider are shown at checkout.

Cancelling

You can cancel a paid plan at any time from your account or by emailing us. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Refunds

If you are not happy, email us within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. After that, payments for a period that has already started are not refundable, except where the law requires it or where we made a mistake, such as a duplicate charge.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks and card networks usually take 5 to 10 working days to show the money.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or charged but did not get access, email us the payment details and we will fix it or refund you.
