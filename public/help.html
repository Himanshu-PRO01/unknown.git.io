Help center

Answers to common questions.

Getting started

What is ToolKit Hub?

A set of free online tools for work and money, such as a GST invoice generator and loan, SIP, FD, PPF and tax calculators.

Do I need an account?

No. All tools work without signing up. An account is only needed for features we add later, such as syncing your details.

Is it free?

Yes. A paid Pro plan with extras such as unlimited invoices and no watermark is planned. See the Pro page.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Every page is built to work on phones, tablets and computers.

Tools

How do I save an invoice as a PDF?

Fill in the invoice, press "Save as PDF", and in the print window choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Why is my result different from my bank's?

Our calculators give estimates using standard formulas. Banks may add fees, round differently or change rates over time.

Which tax regime does the income tax calculator use?

You can choose the new or old regime. It uses the FY 2026-27 slabs and does not include surcharge or special-rate income. Confirm with a chartered accountant.

Where are my saved business details stored?

Only in your own browser, on your device. We do not receive them. You can remove them on the Settings page.

Account and billing

How do I create an account?

Go to Create account, enter your email and a password or use Google, then confirm your email.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password and we will email you a link to choose a new one.

How do I cancel a paid plan?

See Cancel subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Read the Refund Policy, then email us with your payment details.

My payment went through but I still see the free plan.

Wait a few minutes, then see Payment pending. If it is still not active, email us your payment ID.

Privacy and security

Do you store my invoice or loan details?

No. They are calculated in your browser and not sent to us.

How do I clear what the site saved on my device?

Open Settings and choose "Remove all saved data".

How do I report a security problem?

Please follow our Responsible Disclosure page.

For businesses

How do I add a calculator to my website?

Use the widget builder, copy the two lines of code it gives you, and paste them into your site.

Where do my leads go?

Straight from your visitor's browser to the form service you connect, such as Formspree or a Google Sheet. They do not pass through us.
