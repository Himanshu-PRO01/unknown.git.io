How to make a GST invoice

A GST invoice is a bill that shows the tax you charged on goods or services. In India, registered businesses use it so the buyer can claim input tax credit. This free tool helps you put the required details in the right place and work out the tax for you.

What a GST invoice should include

  • Your name, address and GSTIN, and the client's name and address (plus their GSTIN if they are registered).
  • A unique invoice number and the invoice date.
  • A description of each item or service, with its HSN or SAC code, quantity and rate.
  • The taxable value, the GST rate, and the tax amount.
  • The place of supply and the final total.

CGST, SGST or IGST?

If you and your client are in the same state, the tax is split into two equal parts: CGST and SGST. If you are in different states, you charge a single IGST.
