What you may and may not do with our tools and widget.

Last updated: October 2026

Please use ToolKit Hub fairly and lawfully. This policy applies to our website, tools, accounts and the embeddable calculator widget.

You must not

  • Break the law or use the site to help others do so.
  • Try to break, overload or get around the security of the site, or scan it without permission.
  • Use bots or scrapers to copy the site or put a heavy load on it.
  • Upload or send malware, spam or anything harmful.
  • Pretend to be another person or business.
  • Use the widget to mislead people, for example by showing false rates, false guarantees or fake claims about a financial product.
  • Collect personal data through the widget without a lawful reason and a clear privacy notice.
  • Resell or re-brand our tools as your own product without our written permission.

Our calculators give estimates. If you show them to your customers, make clear that results are estimates and not offers.
