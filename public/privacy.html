Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

ToolKit Hub is built to be simple and private. The tools run in your browser, so what you type into them, such as invoice details or loan amounts, is not sent to our servers.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your email address and your message.
  • Information stored on your device. If you use Settings, your saved business details, default currency and cookie choice are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. We do not receive them. You can remove them at any time on the Settings page.
  • Account information. If you create an account, we receive your email address and, if you sign in with Google or another sign-in provider, the basic profile details that provider shares with us. Sign-in is handled by our authentication service provider.
  • Automatic information. Our hosting provider may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and to run the site. Advertising and analytics partners may set cookies, as described below.

3. How we use information

To run and improve the tools, to answer your messages, to keep the site secure, and to show advertising and understand how the site is used.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Personalised ads are shown only if you accept cookies; if you decline, ads are not personalised. You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Analytics

If we enable an analytics service such as Google Analytics, it loads only after you accept cookies and collects usage data such as pages viewed and approximate location.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site (hosting, advertising, analytics) and when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep information

We keep emails only as long as we need them to reply to you and handle your request.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@yourdomain.com. You can change your cookie choice on the Settings page or in your browser. Where laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the GDPR apply to you, you may have additional rights.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the site, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@yourdomain.com.
