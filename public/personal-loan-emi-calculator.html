Common questions

What is an EMI?

EMI means equated monthly instalment. It is the fixed amount you pay your lender each month until the loan is fully repaid. It covers both interest and part of the loan amount.

How accurate is this calculator?

It uses the standard reducing-balance formula, so it is a good estimate. Your lender's figure can differ slightly because of processing fees, rounding, the date of your first instalment, or a rate that changes over time.

What is the difference between a flat rate and a reducing rate?

A flat rate charges interest on the full loan amount for the whole tenure. A reducing rate charges interest only on the balance you still owe, so it costs less. This calculator uses the reducing rate. If a lender quotes a flat rate, ask for the equivalent reducing rate.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to us.

Personal loans in brief

A personal loan is unsecured, which means you do not pledge anything against it. Because of that, the interest rate is usually higher than for home or car loans, and it depends heavily on your credit score and income. Tenures are typically one to five years. The rate shown here is only an example.

Costs to look for

Besides interest, lenders may charge a processing fee, and some charge a fee if you close the loan early. Add these to the EMI result to see the real cost.

Borrow only what you need

Use the calculator to test a smaller amount or a shorter tenure. Paying less interest is usually worth a slightly higher EMI, as long as you can pay it comfortably every month.
