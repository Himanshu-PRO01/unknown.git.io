Data Processing Agreement

Terms for business customers whose data we handle.

Last updated: October 2026

This agreement applies to business customers who use ToolKit Hub paid services, such as the calculator widget, and who ask for these terms. If you need a signed copy, email hello@yourdomain.com.

1. Roles

You are the controller of the personal data of your own visitors and customers. ToolKit Hub acts as a processor only for the data it handles on your behalf.

2. What data we handle

  • Calculator widget: numbers a visitor types are processed in the visitor's browser and are not stored by us. If you turn on the callback form, the visitor's name and phone number are sent from their browser directly to the form service you choose. They do not pass through our servers.

3. Our commitments

  • We process personal data only to provide the services and to follow your written instructions.
  • People with access to it are bound by confidentiality.
  • We use reasonable security measures, described on our Security page.

4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for having a lawful reason to collect your visitors' data, for showing your own privacy notice, and for how you use the form service you connect.

5. Sub-processors

We use these service providers where they apply: a hosting and delivery provider, an authentication provider for accounts, and a payment provider for paid plans. We will update this page if this changes.

6. International transfers

Providers may process data in other countries. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

7. Deletion and return

When your service ends, we delete the personal data we hold for you within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Audits
