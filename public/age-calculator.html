Common questions

How is age calculated?

The calculator counts full years from your date of birth, then the remaining full months and days up to the chosen date.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the "Age on this date" field to any date after your date of birth.

What about leap-year birthdays?

For a 29 February birthday, the next birthday in a non-leap year is shown as 1 March.

About the age calculator

This tool is handy for forms, eligibility dates and planning. It works fully in your browser, and the date you enter is not sent to us.
