What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way.

What we use

Essential and preferenceRemembers your cookie choice and the details you save in Settings (local storage on your device).You can remove them on the Settings page.

How to control cookies

Use the cookie banner or the Settings page to change your choice. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.
