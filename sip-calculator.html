Common questions

What is a SIP?

A systematic investment plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month. It builds the habit of investing regularly and spreads your purchases over time.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Returns depend on the market and the fund, and they can be negative. The return you enter here is only an assumption.

What return should I enter?

Nobody can know future returns. Past performance does not guarantee future results, so it is wise to try a few lower figures as well as an optimistic one.

Do you store my numbers?

No. The calculation runs in your browser.

How a SIP calculator works

A SIP puts the same amount into an investment each month. Because you invest regularly, your earlier returns also earn returns, which is called compounding. The longer you stay invested, the bigger the effect.

The formula

Estimated value = P x [((1 + i)^n - 1) / i] x (1 + i), where P is the monthly amount, i is the monthly rate (the yearly rate divided by 12 and by 100) and n is the number of months.

Keep in mind

This tool is for education and is not investment advice. Read the offer documents of a fund before you invest.
