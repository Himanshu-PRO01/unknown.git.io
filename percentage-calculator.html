Common questions

How do I find X% of Y?

Multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 10% of 5,000 is 5,000 x 10 / 100 = 500.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 250 out of 1,000 is 250 / 1,000 x 100 = 25%.

How is percentage change calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. For example, from 80 to 100 is (100 - 80) / 80 x 100 = 25% increase.
