    Is ToolKit Hub really free?

    Yes. The tools are free to use. A paid Pro plan with extras such as saved history and no watermark is planned for later.

    Do you store my invoices or loan details?

    No. Everything is calculated in your browser. If you save your business details in Settings, they stay on your own device.

    Can I use the invoice for GST filing?

    The invoice generator helps you lay out the details that a GST invoice normally needs. Rules differ for different businesses, so confirm your requirements with a chartered accountant.
